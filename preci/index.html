Overview
Preci is a smart gift-giving app that helps users remember important dates, find the perfect presents, and make gift-giving a joyful, stress-free experience. Designed to act like a personal assistant who truly cares, Preci curates personalized gift ideas, tracks budgets and milestones, and brings creativity back into an often-overwhelming process.


Problem

In today’s fast-paced world, many people struggle to find time and inspiration for thoughtful gifting:

  • Forgetting birthdays, anniversaries, or milestones

  • Overspending on last-minute gifts that feel impersonal

  • Feeling anxious or guilty about not showing appreciation in time

Despite using tools like Google Calendar or Facebook Events, people still scramble to find suitable gifts, turning what should be a warm, expressive act into a source of stress.

How might we make gift-giving simpler, smarter, and more personal?

3. Technical Architecture

  • Unity + Needle Engine: Handles real-time WebXR rendering and multiuser interactions.

  • Generative AI Pipeline: Converts speech → text → image → 3D model using OpenAI, Stability.ai, and custom scripts for latency management.

  • Network Replication: Enables persistent world-state for families to revisit their shared “memory worlds.”

4. Usability Testing

Conducted five remote moderated sessions to evaluate ease of use and flow clarity.

Findings & Fixes:

  • Progress bars were unclear → replaced with labeled step indicators.

  • Confusing “Recipient added” pop-up → redirected users straight to recipient details.

  • Gift progress screen lacked clarity → restructured hierarchy and added voting indicators.

After iteration, testers described the experience as “simple,” “personal,” and “stress-relieving.”

Redesign

Conclusion

As the sole UX designer for this project, I have learned a lot every step of the way. Doing research, conducting usability tests and designing iterations helped me to understand how to design to meet the users’ needs. Initially, I want this product to provide a sense of inspiring, exciting, versatile, creative, caring. It was rewarding to develop these concepts into a user-friendly product. I was not only studying how to create minimalistic UI design through out the process, but also getting the mindset to consider accessibility when making design decisions.  


Looking forward, I would like to keep developing this product with additional user research.It would be nice to have:

          ·  Helping center to provide more instant help
