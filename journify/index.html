Overview
Journify is a multiplayer WebXR journaling platform that uses speech-to-3D generative AI to help parents and children co-create emotional memories in augmented reality.
Created during MIT Reality Hack, Journify reimagines journaling as a collaborative, spatial experience that strengthens family attachment and emotional intelligence through play, storytelling, and shared creativity.


🔥 Problem

Despite the rise of connected technology, parent–child communication is deteriorating.

  • Over 150 million parents report anxiety about losing emotional connection with their children.

  • 2 in 5 kids lack a secure bond with their parents — half of them suffer from mental health challenges as a result.

Families spend thousands on digital entertainment (AR/VR games, apps, and devices), yet none of these tools intentionally nurture attachment or emotional literacy.

How might we design a digital experience that helps families reconnect emotionally, not just interact socially?

Overview
Journify is an augmented reality journaling app that allows users to pin personal memories, photos, and reflections in real-world spaces. Designed during MIT Reality Hack, the experience reimagines journaling as something lived and shared — an emotional layer on top of the physical world.


🔥 Problem

Chinatown’s historic authenticity has been fading under gentrification and generational shifts. Many long-established businesses were forced to close, and younger residents were moving away. Tourists often visited only surface-level attractions, missing the deeper stories behind the neighborhood’s culture and cuisine.

How might design invite visitors to meaningfully engage with Chinatown’s living history — not just consume it?

Process


1. Research & Ideation

  • Synthesized findings from social-emotional learning research (ABFT, RULER).

  • Interviewed parents to understand the gap between emotional connection and tech engagement.

  • Identified a key opportunity: turn journaling into a shared creative ritual that visualizes emotion through co-creation.


    Design Principles

    • Collaborative, not solitary

    • Emotionally expressive, not prescriptive

    • Visually memorable, not abstract

2. Experience Design

  • Each journal prompt is grounded in emotional intelligence research.

  • Each cloud represents a month, with “hanging pages” for new entries.

  • Parents and children record spoken reflections, which are converted through AI into 3D visual artifacts — personalized representations of shared memories.

  • Faded cubes represent past years, forming a spatial archive of family growth.

Through voice-based journaling, players co-create and revisit emotional milestones — a digital scrapbook reimagined in 3D.

3. Technical Architecture

  • Unity + Needle Engine: Handles real-time WebXR rendering and multiuser interactions.

  • Generative AI Pipeline: Converts speech → text → image → 3D model using OpenAI, Stability.ai, and custom scripts for latency management.

  • Network Replication: Enables persistent world-state for families to revisit their shared “memory worlds.”

Conclusion

Journify demonstrated that GenAI and WebXR can strengthen emotional bonds — not replace them.
It showcased the potential of spatial computing as a tool for emotional health, and earned recognition at MIT Reality Hack for combining deep emotional purpose with technical innovation.

The next phase includes pitching Journify as a social-impact startup, building on its 5-year roadmap for R&D, and exploring feature expansions for schools, therapists, and digital wellness programs.
