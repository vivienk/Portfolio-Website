Background & Problem (Why now)
Financial literacy gaps made users rely on customer support to interpret credit score changes. We needed a scalable, friendly, human-centered solution that could provide personalized insights without overwhelming users with financial jargon. TomoIQ is Tomo's AI agent designed to help users understand credit decisions and score changes in plain language — turning confusion into confidence.

The Product decision
I Instead of creating an open-ended chatbot, I focused the first version on three common questions:

  • Why did my credit score change?

  • How is my credit usage affecting me?

  • What should I do next?

TomoIQ connects each explanation to verified account data and a relevant action, such as reviewing a credit report or making a payment.
