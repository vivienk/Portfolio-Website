Takeaways

What I have learned

01

Prompt design made the data architecture tangible

Working with engineering on prompts clarified which data was reliable, timely, and useful enough to shape a customer’s next action. It connected the system behind the response to measurable outcomes and follow-up learning.

02

Tradeoffs are part of the experience

Helpful guidance required explicit boundaries: explain verified changes, keep recommendations proportionate, disclose the source, and escalate outcomes TomoIQ could not safely determine.

03

AI infrastructure creates a learning flywheel

When context, response, CTA, and outcome stay connected, the product learns who it is serving and how to make the next interaction more useful.
