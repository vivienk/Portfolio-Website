Classify before generating

Route by intent, risk, and available context.

Common questions

Score changes, utilization, payment history, and next steps.

Route → Grounded answer
02 · Boundary

Ambiguous requests

Missing or conflicting context.

Route → Clarify or limit
03 · High-risk

Consequential moments

Payments, disputes, privacy, or recommendations.

Route → Confirm or escalate
